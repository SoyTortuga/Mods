Moon Sweepers Twitch Integration - custom Power-ups
====================================================

Each Power-up's title must match PowerUpMappings in the mod's config (capitals don't
matter; spaces and punctuation do). Effects hit every player in the lobby who runs the mod.


Lights Out - 250 Bits
Everyone's helmet lamp switches off and stays off for 15 minutes.

Half Reach - 500 Bits
Everyone's reach for picking up dust canisters is cut in half for 10 minutes.

Refill a Sector - 500 Bits
A random sector the crew already cleared fills back up with dust.

2x Gravity - 750 Bits
Gravity doubles for 15 minutes: falling is twice as strong, and running, jumping and vehicles are at half speed.

Spill The Dust - 1,000 Bits
Everyone drops all the dust they're carrying, scattered on the ground as 1 kg canisters to pick back up.

Fisheye Filter - 1,000 Bits
Everyone's field of view widens to 140° for 15 minutes.

Tunnel Vision - 1,000 Bits
Everyone's field of view narrows to 30° for 15 minutes.

Moon Fog - 1,000 Bits
A grey fog rolls in for 10 minutes, cutting visibility to about 35 m.

Tiny Pockets - 1,250 Bits
Everyone can carry only half as much dust, on foot and in vehicles, for 15 minutes. Dust already carried is kept.

4x Gravity - 1,500 Bits
Gravity is 4x stronger for 15 minutes, and running, jumping and vehicles are at a quarter speed.

Potato Mode - 1,500 Bits
The game renders at a quarter of its resolution for 15 minutes.

Roomba Shutdown - 2,000 Bits
Every Roomba switches off and heads back to its dock until someone switches it back on.

Back to Square One - 2,500 Bits
Everyone's brush is swapped for the starter toothbrush for 15 minutes, then given back.

Australian Moon - 2,500 Bits
Everyone's view turns upside down for 10 minutes. The HUD stays upright.

8x Gravity - 3,000 Bits
Gravity is 8x stronger for 15 minutes, and running, jumping and vehicles are at an eighth speed.

JOONAS Shutdown - 3,000 Bits
J.O.O.N.A.S. switches off. Someone has to find it and switch it back on.

Shrink-Flation - 3,000 Bits
Pay for every kg of dust and every finished sector is cut in half for 30 minutes.

Inverted Controls - 3,000 Bits
Movement and camera controls are reversed for 10 minutes.

Vehicle Breakdown - 5,000 Bits
Everyone is thrown out of their vehicle and can't get back in for 15 minutes.

Mercy - 5,000 Bits
Ends every active timed effect right away.

Bankruptcy - 10,000 Bits
The crew's shared money drains to $0.


Notes
-----
- A stronger gravity Power-up replaces a weaker one and restarts the 15 minutes, and the
  same one used again restarts the timer. A weaker one used while a stronger one is
  running does nothing, so viewers may want to know before they spend Bits on it.
- Roomba Shutdown and JOONAS Shutdown haven't been tested in a save with robots yet.
